42110 Land Rover Defender, v5 (with 4L motors and RC electronics)

 Highlights
 2025-02-23 

This is an updated version (v5) of my Land Rover Defender 42110 with RC electronics and 4 L motors for driving. In this version, I significantly improved the strength of the chassis and the gearbox, and finally achieved what I had long been aiming for — the model is now durable and capable enough to play with outdoors.

Specs

  • Driving: 4 PF Red L motors
  • Steering: Geek Servo motor
  • Two-speed gearbox with a center differential locked in low gear. Controlled by a Geek Servo motor.
  • Open differentials in axles
  • Planetary wheel hubs
  • Lifted suspension
  • 90 mm RC tyres
  • Winch: 1 CaDA micromotor
  • No fake engine
  • Working steering wheel
  • Custom LED lights
  • Powered by 3S LiPo (3x14500)
  • Turning diameter (by wheels):
    • 1st gear (central diff closed): 110cm
    • 2nd gear (central diff open): 108cm
  • Curb weight: 2.2 kg
