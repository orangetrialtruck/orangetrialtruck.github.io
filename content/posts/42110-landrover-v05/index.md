This is an updated version (v5) of my [Land Rover Defender 42110]({{< ref "/posts/42110-landrover-v04" >}}) with RC electronics and 4 L motors for driving. In this version, I significantly improved the strength of the chassis and the gearbox, and finally achieved what I had long been aiming for — the model is now durable and capable enough to play with outdoors. 

<!--more-->

## Specs
* Driving: 4 PF Red L motors
* Steering: Geek Servo motor
* Two-speed gearbox with a center differential locked in low gear. Controlled by a Geek Servo motor.
* Open differentials in axles
* Planetary wheel hubs
* Lifted suspension
* 90 mm RC tyres
* Winch: 1 CaDA micromotor
* No fake engine
* Working steering wheel
* Custom LED lights
* Powered by 3S LiPo (3x14500)
* Turning diameter (by wheels):
	* 1st gear (central diff closed): 110cm
	* 2nd gear (central diff open): 108cm
* Curb weight: 2.2 kg

### Gearing: 

| Gear | Central diff | Gears | Final ratio |
| ---- | -----------  |----|-------|
| 1st  | Locked       | 12:20 -> 20:12 -> 20:28 (diff in axles) -> Planetary hubs | 7.56:1 |
| 2nd  | Open         | 16:16 -> 20:12 -> 20:28 (diff in axles) -> Planetary hubs | 4.53:1 |
