MOC - Ford Bronco RC v1.0. LEGO Technic, RC electronics FlySky, 4L motors.

<!--more-->

## Specs
* Driving: 4 PF Red L motors
* Steering: Geek Servo motor
* Two-speed gearbox with a center differential locked in low gear. Controlled by a Geek Servo motor.
* Open front differential
* Lockable rear differential (GeekServo)
* Planetary wheel hubs
* 90 mm RC tyres
* No fake engine
* Custom LED lights
* Powered by 3S LiPo (3x14500)
* Curb weight: 1.8 kg

### Gearing: 

| Gear | Central diff | Final ratio |
| ---- | -----------  |-------|
| 1st  | Locked       | 8.48:1 |
| 2nd  | Open         | 5.09:1 |
